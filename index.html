Icebergs are like prose poems that glaciers compose for the sea. They break away from the ice shelves, carrying memories of millennia on a silent journey. Only one-tenth of their mass floats above water; the rest lies hidden in the deep, mysterious blue. 冰山，是冰川写给大海的散文诗。它们从冰架剥离，携着万年光阴的记忆，开始一场沉默的流浪。海面之上的皎洁，仅是它生命的十分之一；更多的部分，是沉潜于幽蓝深处的、不为人知的庞大与深邃。

They drift gently with the wind and currents, graceful like ancient messengers. Sometimes they crack and thunder, telling their story; sometimes they remain silent, reflecting the sky and auroras. In the end, they return everything to the ocean, completing a grand and tranquil cycle. 它随风与洋流缓缓漂移，姿态从容如远古来的使者。有时崩裂轰鸣，是它在诉说；有时静默不语，倒映着天空与极光。最终，它将一切归还于海洋，完成一场盛大而安静的轮回。

A majestic snow-capped mountain rises ahead, its peaks glinting silver in the sunlight. Glaciers slowly extend from the slopes down to the sea, as if bridging sky and ocean. 一座雄伟的雪山出现在前方，阳光下峰顶闪烁着银色光芒。冰川缓缓从山坡延伸至海边，仿佛架起了天空与海洋的桥梁。

The boat approaches the shore. Feel the firmness and chill of the icy ground beneath your feet. Each step is like entering an unknown wonder, while enjoying the unique grandeur of the polar landscape. 船缓缓靠近岸边，准备登岸。感受脚下冰冷坚实的地面，每一步都像踏入未知的奇观，同时欣赏极地景观独特的壮丽。

On this timeless white continent, penguins are the most elegant commas, pausing between ice and sky. They are not clumsy visitors, but true poets of the icy realm, writing resilience with their black-and-white plumage. 在这片被时间遗忘的纯白大陆上，它们是最优雅的逗号，停顿在冰原与天空之间。南极的企鹅，不是笨拙的过客，而是冰雪王国真正的诗人，用黑白相间的羽衣书写着生命的韧性。

When the polar sun strikes the ice at an angle, they begin their daily pilgrimage—marching toward the sea like gentlemen in formal attire attending an ancient rendezvous, or standing silently on the ice cliffs, gazing afar, reflecting the solitude of the Southern Hemisphere. 当极昼的阳光以最倾斜的角度抚摸冰面，它们便开始了每日的朝圣——或是列队走向海洋，如身着礼服的绅士赴一场古老的约会；或是静立冰崖，凝望远方，眼中盛装着整个南半球的孤独。

Emperor penguins incubate hope in -40°C winds, keeping their eggs on their feet and using their body heat to endure the harsh polar climate. Their huddling forms are the warmest paradox in Antarctica, radiating the fiercest emotions in the coldest places. 帝企鹅在零下四十度的寒风中孵化希望，将蛋置于脚上，以身体的温度对抗极地的严酷。那相互依偎的身影，是南极最温暖的悖论——在最寒冷处，绽放最炽热的情感。

Adélie penguins, like icy sprites, leap from the water, sending sparkling droplets into the air and tracing graceful arcs against the blue ice. They carefully select stones to build nests; these gray-brown pebbles become precious treasures in the barren land. 阿德利企鹅则像是冰雪世界的精灵，从水中跃出时带起碎钻般的水花，在蓝冰衬托下划出优美的弧线。它们用喙精心挑选石子筑巢，那些灰褐色的卵石，是荒原上最珍贵的宝石。

When the aurora dances across the sky, they stand upright, as if listening to the secret language of the universe. In their ancient postures lies an eternal story of survival, reproduction, and perseverance. These are Antarctica's penguins—a beautiful poem in the snowstorm, the most touching fable of life on this silent continent. 当极光在天幕起舞，它们仰首静立，仿佛在聆听宇宙的秘语。万年如一的姿态里，藏着关于生存、繁衍与坚持的永恒叙事。这是南极的企鹅——风雪中的美好诗篇。沉默大陆上最动人的生命寓言。

The Antarctic research station sits on the ice sheet, serving as an essential base for scientists conducting polar research and environmental monitoring. 南极科考站坐落在冰盖上，是科学家开展极地研究和环境监测的重要基地。

Equipped with advanced laboratories and living facilities, scientists stay here long-term, observing climate changes, wildlife behavior, and glacial movements. 科考站配备先进的实验室和生活设施，科学家们长期驻守于此，观察气候变化、野生动物行为以及冰川运动。

Every recorded measurement and every footprint in the snow contributes to polar science and bears witness to history. 每一次记录的测量数据，每一处雪地的足迹，都为极地科学贡献力量，也见证着历史。

No camera or image can capture even one percent of an iceberg's grandeur, and words cannot convey even a millionth of the polar light. 镜头和画面带不走冰山百分之一的壮美，文字写不出极地光芒的千万分之一。

Only by experiencing it firsthand can one understand why this white continent is called Earth's last frontier. 唯有亲历，方能懂得这片白色大陆为何被称为地球最后的秘境。
